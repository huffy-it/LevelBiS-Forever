1. Item sources (drops, quest rewards, vendors): QuestieDB
----------------------------------------------------------

Where each item comes from (the NPCs that drop it, the quests that reward it,
the vendors that sell it, and their levels, zones and factions) is derived
from QuestieDB by the Questie team:

2. Item stats: World of Warcraft: Forever client tables
--------------------------------------------------------

Item names, stats, armor, weapon damage, requirements and set bonuses are read
from the game's client tables (build 1.60.1.70862, with hotfixes) as published
by wago.tools (https://wago.tools). World of Warcraft is a trademark of
Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.; LevelBiS Forever is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
